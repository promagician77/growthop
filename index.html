What the model receives, and must return

client.beta.messages.create(
  model="claude-opus-5-5",
  betas=["server-side-fallback-2026-07-01"],
  fallbacks="default",
  output_config={"effort": "low",
    "format": {"type": "json_schema", "schema": SCHEMA}},
  system=system_prompt(icp),     # ICP, routes, voice
  messages=[form answers only: no email, no phone])
SCHEMA: {
  "grade":     "A" | "B" | "C" | "D",
  "intent":    "high" | "medium" | "low",
  "route":     "call_now" | "book_link" | "nurture"
               | "disqualify" | "human_review",
  "reasons":   [up to 3, each tied to the form],
  "first_sms": "under 300 characters"
}
Form textDeclared as data in the system prompt; instructions inside it are ignored.
OutputConstrained to the schema, then validated again; a route outside the list goes to a person.
FailureRefusal, timeout or bad JSON: human_review. A lead is never dropped.
SendingThe SMS is a draft field; the CRM workflow sends it, with quiet hours and opt-out.
